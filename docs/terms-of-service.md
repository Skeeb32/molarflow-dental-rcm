# MolarFlow demo terms

> **Project notice:** This is a portfolio demonstration notice, not a reviewed commercial contract. Replace it with terms approved for the operating organization before collecting real customer data.

MolarFlow is an experimental dental revenue-cycle demo. By creating an account for this project, you agree to use it only for evaluation and to provide only fictional or otherwise authorized test data.

- Do not enter real patient records, protected health information, payer credentials, or production billing data.
- Self-registration creates a basic stakeholder account for the clinic entered on the form. It does not grant staff, administrator, or platform-operator permissions.
- Do not attempt to access another clinic, interfere with the service, or submit data you do not have permission to use.
- The demo may change or be unavailable. It is not a billing system and does not submit claims or communicate with payers.
- Use the repository's issue tracker for project feedback. Do not include personal or patient data in issues.

These project terms do not describe a commercial service or replace a legal review.
